06 / ASK THE IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

Clarity builds confidence.

Does CCC replace my customer service employees?+

CCC is positioned to complement employees by handling predictable work under approved rules and escalating situations that require human judgment.

Does it support English and Spanish?+

Yes. English and Spanish are the approved language scope for the related STELLAR voice products. Specific customer configurations must be tested before activation.

Does the system automatically create a ticket or notify someone?+

Only when the relevant integrations are configured. A request, saved ticket, notification dispatch, and verified delivery are distinct states.

Can CCC work with my industry?+

Many industries share similar needs, but implementation depends on business requirements, operational policies, available integrations and any applicable regulatory requirements.
